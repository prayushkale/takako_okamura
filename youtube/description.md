Takako Okamura: Complete Discography Digest (1985–2019)

This discography video traces Takako Okamura's 35-year career—spanning 52 releases from her 1985 solo debut through 2019—by presenting highlights (focusing on the chorus) of one song from each release in chronological order.

Journey through her career: from her debut with "Kaze wa Umi kara"; the smash hit "Haguresou na Tenshi" (featured in the Honda "Today" commercial); the enduringly beloved "Yume wo Akiramenaide" (a favorite across both singles and albums); all the way to her 18th studio album, *fierte*. Experience the evolution of her sound in a single video—from the sparkling energy of the 80s to the artistic maturity of the 2010s.
